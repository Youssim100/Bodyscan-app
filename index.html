          Settings

          Appearance
          Session length includes

          On: the opening or closing fits inside the length you choose. Off: it's added on top, so body parts get the full length.

          Interruptions

          • Calls, Siri or other audio: the session pauses. Tap Resume when you're ready.
          • Switching apps or locking the phone: the session pauses and waits for you. The screen stays on during a session.
          • Notifications: banners don't stop the session.
          • No internet: everything, including all voices, is stored on your phone, so sessions play offline.
